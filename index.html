How To Mix & Formulate

1

Select Particle Micron

Choose temperature trigger (e.g., 31°C body heat) and compatible micron distribution for liquid resin, ink, or polymer extrusion.

2

Blend into Clear Binder

Disperse at 1% to 5% by weight into neutral coatings, epoxy resins, acrylics, textile silkscreen inks, or plastisol.

3

Instant Thermal/UV Reaction

Observe instant transition when applied onto automotive panels, apparel, mugs, packaging, or security anti-counterfeiting seals.
